Brick Your Finals

A sales-focused campaign plan for Brick, a $59 device that locks distracting apps until you tap your phone on it again. I'm building it on my own, from research to creative.

Why Brick, why students

Brick is a small Wisconsin startup founded in 2023. You choose which apps to block, tap your phone on the device to lock them, and tap again to unlock. Because the device stays wherever you left it, unlocking takes a deliberate walk instead of one tap on the screen. It costs $59 once, with no subscription.

Its co-founder says Brick's biggest customer group is people aged 20 to 35. College students sit right inside that group, and finals season is when they most need to focus.

The gap in the market

Most screen-time tools live on the phone itself, so the person trying to stop is also the one holding the off-switch. Brick moves the unlock off the phone.

Off-switch on the phone

Apple Screen Time, Forest, Opal, Freedom

One tap to give in.

Off-switch in the room

Brick (and Padlock, a smaller UK device)

A walk to give in.

The idea

"Tap. It's a Brick."

The tap is the product, so every part of the campaign lets students see it, try it and share it.
