I joined ChinaVenture to help prepare the 18th China Venture Investment Conference, a gathering for the PE/VC community.

In the weeks before the event, I wrote bios for the confirmed speakers, worked with the design team to finalize their posters, and published more than 15 countdown and speaker posts on the company's WeChat account to build anticipation. During the same period, members of ChinaVenture's research team spoke at other industry events, and I wrote three news releases covering what they shared.

On the day of the conference, I helped with on-site operations, welcoming guests and guiding them through the venue.

I was also a point of contact for the media throughout the conference, working with more than 10 financial and industry outlets. Before the event, I coordinated with them on their preview stories to help build early interest. On the day, I confirmed which journalists and publications were attending and checked in with them on site. Afterward, as recap and highlight stories came in, I reviewed photos and details with reporters before publication and compiled the coverage once it ran, more than 30 placements in total.
