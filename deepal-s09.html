Deepal S09 on Xiaohongshu

Account Executive Intern · Beijing D&S Consulting · Jul – Sep 2025

Campaign
Changan Deepal S09, phase two
My platform
Xiaohongshu (RedNote)
Output
15+ UGC-style posts written

I joined D&S as the Changan Deepal S09 campaign moved past its launch phase, which had focused on specs, pricing and offers. The second phase centered on creator and UGC content across Douyin, Bilibili, WeChat Channels and Xiaohongshu, alongside competitor comparison articles.

I worked on Xiaohongshu, writing image-and-text posts for smaller creator accounts. The goal was UGC-style content: posts that read like a real owner's experience rather than an ad. I started from each account's persona. For a mom creator, I wrote everyday scenes such as the school run, a family camping trip and a day caring for a sick child, and let the car's features appear where they naturally mattered: the spacious cabin, comfortable seats, the movable center console, the rear-seat screen, and a fridge that can both warm and cool.

I also helped compile the team's weekly reports, putting audience feedback and content results into slides. It gave me an early look at how a campaign is measured and adjusted along the way.
